Terms of Service

Ademi Clips is a private, non-commercial tool run by one streamer on his own computer. It turns highlights from his own Twitch streams into short vertical videos and uploads them to his own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

Who can use it

The tool is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no other TikTok users sign in to it.

Content

  • All uploaded videos come from the owner's own live streams.
  • The owner is responsible for every video that is posted and makes sure it follows the TikTok Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • The tool does not post, like, comment or follow on behalf of anyone else.

Availability

The tool is provided as is. It may change or stop at any time.
