Privacy Policy

What data is used

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the owner (display name, open ID) to confirm which account is connected.
  • An access token from TikTok, so the tool can upload videos to the owner's account.
  • The owner's own stream recordings, which are processed into short videos.

Where data is stored

Tokens and videos are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Third-party services

Videos are uploaded to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs. A short text description of each clip is generated with an AI model (Anthropic Claude) to write titles and captions.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Local tokens and videos can be deleted from the computer at any time.
